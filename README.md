# StudySpot

**A personalized cafe recommendation system for students, workers, and coffee connoisseurs.**

## Overview

StudySpot is a desktop application that helps users find cafes matching their personal preferences. Unlike typical platforms like Google Maps and Yelp that mainly rely on ratings and lengthy customer reviews, this app considers and summarizes other crucial factors such as noise level, lighting, cleanliness, comfort, outlet availability, and food and beverage quality.

Users can search for nearby cafes and customize their search according to their preferences. They may also rate the cafe across multiple aspects and receive further recommendations based on their needs.

## Targeted Users

This application is for anyone in search of customizable cafe recommendations but primary users would include:

- Students
- Remote workers
- Cafe hoppers / drink enthusiasts

## Personal Interest

As a university student constantly in search of good cafes to study in and someone who loves a well-made matcha and pastry, I was driven to create StudySpot in the absence of a platform that could accurately find a cafe suitable for my needs. Rather than relying on generic 1-5 star ratings that do not provide much information on the cafe itself (and thus having to comb through many written reviews that may not contain the information I am looking for), I wanted to create an application that actually focuses on an individual user's preferences rather than those of the general public.

## User Stories

- As a student studying for an exam, I want to find a quiet, well-lit cafe with Wi-Fi and outlets so I can comfortably review my notes for several hours.
- As an avid coffee drinker, I want to find and rate cafes based on their drink quality and ambience so I can remember which places align with my preferences.
